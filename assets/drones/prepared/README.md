# Prepared sci-fi drone

`scifi-drone.json` is generated by `yarn assets:prepare` from the unmodified CC0 Innerscene model
in `../original/scifi-drone.glb`. Source hash, source URL, licence and modification notes are
included in the generated file.

- One non-indexed 360-triangle mesh, with source textures sampled into linear vertex colours.
- Four blade pivots reconstructed about their motor axes, rather than the zero node origins.
- Per-vertex pivot XYZ and spin direction; the shader rotates vertices and normals around Y.
- A body-centred frame with Y up and -Z forward; source metre scale is preserved.
- One authored 36-triangle box/arm silhouette for distance rendering.
- A conservative 1.027 m culling radius covers the full rotor sweep.

The 36-triangle silhouette is deliberately coarse. The original source files remain unchanged.
Rotor geometry/bounds/provenance are covered by `tools/sky/asset.test.mjs`; GPU flight comparisons
and the browser demo are separate checks. No physics, collision or real-world flight claim follows
from these assets.


## Additional runtime designs

`yarn assets:prepare` also verifies and prepares `quadcopter-original.json` and
`inspection-drone.json`. Both preserve original source hashes/CC0 provenance, merge materials
into linear vertex colours, encode four rotor pivots and include matching silhouettes.
Full/low triangles: quadcopter 5,036/84; inspection 1,252/148. Original +Z fronts are rotated to -Z;
uniform demo scaling and gear alignment fit the original 1.027 m envelope with a -0.35 m contact
plane. Inspection guards stay static while its four extracted blade boxes spin. Source dimensions
are not changed in the original GLBs. Each generated JSON records its exact transform and origins.
See decision 0014 for fixed-ID assignment, draw strategy and pending GPU/performance acceptance.
